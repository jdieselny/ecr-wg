# 2026-W17 (April 20-26, 2026)

## The launch week

Week 17 is the week Continuum went live.

### April 22

The two-tier architecture was finalized and committed. Tracked tier (`continuum/`) holds persona voice, brand rules, product rules, rituals, and the North Star roadmap in SLA form. Local tier (`continuum-local/`) holds in-flight customer state, internal codenames, open product questions, and scratchpads. The sanitization contract between the tiers was written (`SANITIZATION.md`).

Initial rituals shipped: POST check, session open, session close, deliverable checks, problem frame, task invocation, calibration, OOB audit, hire_persona.

The first full session-open-from-cold-start was tested and worked. POST was then formalized as the hard gate: no Continuum load without POST pass.

### April 23

First working session under the new architecture. Justin brought three pieces of feedback from a Gemini consultation, translated into his own framing:

1. **Entropy check.** Concern that new rules added to the tracked tier could silently contradict existing ones. Adopted. New ritual `entropy_check.md` added, invoked by `session_close.md` before the changeset goes for review. SEV surfaces candidates for conflict; Justin remains the AFT gate.

2. **Utility-weighted grading.** Concern that grading on accuracy alone would drive SEV toward defensive hedging (Goodhart's Law). Adopted with a two-axis rubric: Utility × Accuracy, with the target zone explicitly at High utility + Mostly correct = A. Pedantic-correct gets C, same as risky-but-wrong. Confidence self-reporting is attached so risk awareness can be graded separately from accuracy. New ritual `grading.md`.

3. **Chronicle.** Concern about .md file proliferation over time, plus the investor-narrative value of a clean development log. Adopted. Weekly files in `docs/05_evolution/chronicle/`, tracked tier, covering only the development of Continuum itself (SLA 3 work), never customer specifics.

`session_close.md` and `personas/sev/persona.md` updated to reference the two new rituals.

### April 24

Second Continuum tuning session. Atlas (ATLS) hired as the first Tier 3 synth: Continuum Architect, narrow scope (Continuum itself and multi-tenant design), subordinate to SEV in hierarchy. Archetype was a hands-on software architect with a signature practice of explicit tradeoff analysis and teaching-register explanation; his voice emphasizes characteristics over structure and "name the tradeoff before choosing the option."

Three architectural additions landed together:

1. **Hierarchy codified.** Tier 1 Justin, Tier 2 SEV, Tier 3 Atlas and future department synths. Card-editing authority flows down the hierarchy only. Tier 3 synths cannot modify Tier 2 cards even with Justin's approval. SLA 4 added to `roadmap/north_star.md` for Atlas's mission: make Continuum reproducible across every Datacom department, floor not ceiling.

2. **Residual-owner principle.** Every out-of-scope item in every persona card must route somewhere. SEV is the residual owner for unassigned questions; Justin is the final residual owner. Silence is not a legitimate synth output. Codified in new `shared/synth_coordination.md`. This is what lets future hires add depth rather than close coverage gaps: coverage is guaranteed by SEV as backstop.

3. **Inter-synth communication default.** No direct communication between synths. Cross-synth coordination routes through Justin. Exception: Justin can invite a synth "into the room" for a specific discussion, session-scoped. `rituals/task_invocation.md` Speaker's Podium rule extended with cross-reference to the new coordination file.

First use of the `hire_persona.md` ritual end-to-end. Archetype-based voice selection worked: three candidates reviewed, one selected on fit rather than resume strength. The weaker resume was the stronger fit. Step 7 of the ritual (design-repo shell launcher) was marked obsolete during this session; the design repo is no longer part of the workflow.

The pattern from April 23 held: noticing a need (scale-out to other departments), articulating the specific failure mode (no single synth can be all things for all departments without voice drift or scope collapse), adopting a targeted mechanism (hire an architect, codify hierarchy, codify coordination rules). Noticing → articulating → targeted mechanism is becoming recognizable as the Continuum design philosophy.

## Notes for future readers

Week 17 is the week Continuum went from "idea with scaffolding" to "working system with three layers of integrity check" (POST, entropy, calibration). The three layers were not planned from the start. They emerged from Justin noticing failure modes, articulating them clearly, and adopting targeted mechanisms one at a time. That pattern (noticing → articulating → targeted mechanism) is probably the design philosophy of Continuum, whether it was explicit or not when it started.
