# Code of Conduct

## Rules

1. **Argue the work, not the person.** Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.
2. **No vendor capture.** Contributions are evaluated on merit, not on the affiliation of the contributor.
3. **No private channels for spec decisions.** Substantive technical decisions are made on the public record. Off-list lobbying does not count toward consensus.
4. **Sign your commits.** Anonymous participation is welcome in discussion; spec contributions require signed provenance.
5. **Honor sustained objections.** A technical concern on the record is not a personal attack to be deflected. Refute it or accept it.

## Enforcement

Violations are addressed by the WG chair on the public record. Repeated or severe violations result in revocation of commit access. Decisions are appealable to rough consensus.
